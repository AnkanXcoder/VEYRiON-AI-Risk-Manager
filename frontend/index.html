Evidence, not invented meaning
The explanation is deliberately limited to what the benchmark supports.
V1–V28 are anonymized/transformed model features.

VEYRiON does not claim that V14 means "merchant risk", V10 means "device risk", or any other unsupported business interpretation. SHAP only tells us how strongly each model feature contributed to this prediction.
Production path:
Indian payment deployment would require richer payment, merchant, customer, device, velocity, and location context. This prototype does not pretend to have those fields.

A useful risk manager must consider both sides of the error.
